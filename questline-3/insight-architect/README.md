## KEY INSIGHTS

1. Maths score- most people scored in the middle
 Most people scored somewhere       between 50-80 in maths. Very few got really high or low marks with slightly more people trailing off on the low side than high side.

2. Prep course is usefull
Students who did the prep course scored higher than those who didnt. This is true for all subjects.

3. Parents' education level matters for reading scores
Students whose parents hsve a masters or bachelor's degree tended to get better reading scores. students whose parents only finished hig school tended to score a bit less.

4. If you're good at math, you're probably good at reading too
When you plot every student's math score against their reading score, there's a clear pattern: points go up and to the right. Meaning students who scored well in math usually also scored well in reading.

5. Reading and writing go together the most 
Comparing all 3 subjects : reading and writing scores are very strongly linked. Maths is also linked to both but just a little less strongly.

6. Few students scored low across all 3 subjects
18 students scored less than 40 in all 3 subjects: supporting the pattern suggested by the histogram's left tail and the scatter plot's lower-left cluster, and consistent with the strong positive correlations seen in the heatmap.
